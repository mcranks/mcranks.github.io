Titles

  • Legend++ Special title for homage players too importants for the community.
  • Legend+ The highest rank, achieved by the most skilled players.
  • Legend A prestigious rank for top-tier players.
  • Master An advanced rank for experienced players.
  • Pro A professional rank for highly skilled players.
  • Intermediate A rank for players with moderate skills.
  • Beginner The starting rank for new players.

To get any of these titles, you need to meet the requirements.

Title Requirements

  • Legend++ No requirements (special players)
  • Legend+ 8.60+ pts
  • Legend 8.00+ pts
  • Master 7.40+ pts
  • Pro 6.40+ pts
  • Intermediate 4.80+ pts
  • Beginner 0.67+ pts

How to get ranked

To get forced ranked, you need to upload your clip in any social media, showing your nickname, mods folder and Mod Menu at the game
